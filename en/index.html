How it works

Three steps — then it runs on its own

VoiceGrep sits quietly in your menu bar and kicks in on its own. You just talk.

1

Notices the call

Detects when a conversation starts and ends and records it automatically. System audio and your microphone are recorded on two separate tracks.

2

Transcribes on your Mac

After the call, the audio is turned into an accurate transcript right on the device, using a local CoreML speech engine for English and Russian.

3

Recognizes people

Name someone once and their voiceprint is saved — from then on, their lines are labeled with their name in every meeting.

Features

Not just a recorder — a memory of your conversations

From automatic recording to an assistant that answers questions across your whole archive.

Automatic recording

Starts and stops with the call. Back-to-back calls are split into separate meetings.

Bring your own recordings

Already have a recording? Just import it: audio or video from any app is transcribed the same way — with speaker names, a summary and tasks.

On-device transcription

Accurate transcripts after every call, produced by a local CoreML engine. Neither the audio nor the text ever leaves your Mac.

Voice recognition

Name a speaker once — their voiceprint is saved and picks them out in future meetings.

Captures only the call

When recording automatically, only the call app’s audio makes it into the track — notifications, music and pings from other apps stay out.

Bookmarks & screenshots

An optional floating panel while you record: timer, marks (important, question, task, decision) and screenshots — each pinned to its moment in the conversation.

Summaries & tasks

Optional post-processing: a meeting summary and a task list with owners. Tasks are suggested — you confirm the ones that matter.

Archive assistant

Ask about your whole archive, a person, a project or a single meeting: “what did we agree on?”, “what tasks did Sam give me?” Answers link back to the conversations.

People & contact graph

A card for everyone you talk to: role, company, notes, an auto-profile built from what they said, and a visual graph of who meets with whom.

Export & transfer

Export any meeting to Markdown (summary, tasks, transcript). People’s voiceprints can be moved between your Macs.

Nothing gets lost

A meeting is saved from the very first second and survives failures: after a crash, the recording is recovered and transcribed. Automatic backups run regularly.

Private by default

Your conversations stay yours

Recording, transcription and voice recognition run entirely on your Mac. Audio, transcripts and voiceprints are never sent anywhere — this isn’t a setting you could switch off by accident; it’s how the app is built.

Post-processing and the assistant via an external LLM are a separate option you turn on yourself, with your own key.

  • No cloudAudio and text never leave the device. After a one-time model download, it works offline.

Install

Up and running in a minute

The app isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so the first launch takes one step in Terminal.

  1. Open the .dmg and drag VoiceGrep into your Applications folder.
  2. Clear the Gatekeeper quarantine — open Terminal and run:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/VoiceGrep.app
  3. Launch VoiceGrep. The first-run setup asks for access to the Microphone and Screen Recording.
  4. For calls in English, open Settings → Транскрипция (Transcription) → Язык встреч (Meeting language) and pick English or Автоопределение (Auto-detect). The default is Russian, which won’t transcribe English speech correctly; the setting applies to new transcriptions.

From then on the app updates itself — no need to repeat the workaround.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where do my recordings and transcripts go?

Nowhere. Recording, transcription and voice recognition run entirely on your Mac. Audio, text and voiceprints are stored locally only and are never sent to the cloud.

Which languages does it transcribe?

English and Russian — choose one in Settings or let the app auto-detect. The speech engine runs locally on CoreML. Voice recognition doesn’t depend on the language at all.

Is the app itself in English?

Not fully yet. The interface is in Russian, and summaries, tasks and meeting titles are generated in Russian too. Transcribing English calls works fully — just switch the meeting language to English (step 4 of the install). The assistant can answer in English: Settings → Ассистент (Assistant) → Язык ответа (Answer language) → English.

What permissions does it need?

Microphone and Screen Recording — the first-run setup asks for both. Screen Recording is how macOS lets an app capture the call’s system audio; no screen video is ever saved.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only once, on first launch, to download the speech model (about 0.6–1 GB). After that, recording, transcription and recognition work offline. The internet is otherwise used only for auto-updates and for the optional external-LLM post-processing, if you turn it on with your own key.

Which Macs are supported?

Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and later) running macOS 14.4 or newer. The download is about 23 MB.
