Privacy

VoiceGrep and the “VoiceGrep — who is speaking” browser extension. Updated October 26, 2026.

In short

VoiceGrep is a Mac app that records calls and transcribes them on your device. Recordings, transcripts and voiceprints stay on your Mac. We run no servers that receive your conversations, and we do not sell or share your data.

The VoiceGrep app

  • Audio, transcripts, participants and voiceprints are stored in the app's folder on your Mac.
  • Speech and voice recognition run on the device. Recognition models are downloaded on first launch; none of your data is sent with that.
  • If you connect a cloud language-model provider in Settings (for summaries, tasks, the assistant), transcript text is sent to the provider you chose, under its terms. Without such a connection, text never leaves the Mac.
  • If you turn on the team directory, people's names and voiceprints are published to the git repository you configured — and only there.
  • Updates are checked against a public update feed; the request carries none of your recordings.

The “VoiceGrep — who is speaking” browser extension

  • What it reads. Only on call pages of supported services (currently Kontur.Talk): participants' display names on the tiles and the speaking indicator. It does not read chat, screen, audio, other sites or your browsing history.
  • Where it sends it. Only to the VoiceGrep app on the same Mac, through the browser's built-in channel to local programs (native messaging). The extension sends nothing to the internet — not to us, not to third parties.
  • When. Names are passed only while VoiceGrep is recording the call. No recording — names never leave the browser.
  • What it stores. Only your settings (consent and the list of enabled services) in browser storage.
  • Why. So that after transcription VoiceGrep can label the voices with the right names. A name set from a hint is marked as such and can be corrected or undone.
  • The extension asks for consent on first run; you can turn it off at any time in the extension itself or by removing it from the browser.

The use of information received by the extension adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: the data is used only for this feature, is not transferred to third parties, is not used for advertising and is not read by humans.
